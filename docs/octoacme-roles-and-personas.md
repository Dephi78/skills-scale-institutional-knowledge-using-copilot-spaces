# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define testable acceptance criteria and manage the QA process.

### Responsibilities
- Define and document test strategy and acceptance criteria for features
- Create and maintain test plans and QA checklists
- Coordinate manual QA and acceptance testing efforts
- Identify quality gaps and advocate for necessary improvements
- Track defects and coordinate resolution with development team
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Maintain clear, testable acceptance criteria throughout execution

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria definition sessions with Product Managers and Developers
- Test status updates during standups and sprint reviews
- Defect reports and resolution coordination with Developers
- Pre-release QA signoff and smoke test verification

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria clarity; coordinate defect resolution and retesting
- **Product Managers**: Validate that features meet user expectations and acceptance criteria
- **Project Managers**: Provide QA status updates and identify quality-related risks or delays

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders or project sponsors represent business interests and provide strategic context, approvals, and success metrics for projects. They ensure that projects deliver business value and align with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and high-level objectives
- Define and validate success metrics and business outcomes
- Provide strategic context and business priorities
- Approve major trade-offs and scope changes
- Communicate project status to leadership and affected teams
- Support escalation and risk mitigation when needed

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Reduce business risk and stakeholder surprises

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status briefings
- Milestone reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Escalation and decision-making forums

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates and seek approvals for trade-offs or scope changes
- **Product Managers**: Align on business value, success metrics, and strategic priorities
- **Developers/Team**: Communicate business context and success criteria

---

## Tech Lead/Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Tech Leads or Architecture Owners guide technical direction, design decisions, and system scalability. They ensure that implementations align with technical standards and reduce technical debt.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Review proposed solutions for scalability, maintainability, and alignment with standards
- Identify and advocate for technical debt reduction
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Coordinate cross-component integration and dependencies
- Support technical risk identification and mitigation

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable technical implementations
- Reduce technical debt and system fragility
- Maintain consistency across codebase and architecture

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Architecture and design decision documentation
- Code review and mentoring discussions
- Technical risk escalation and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, review designs, and mentor on implementation
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and mitigation timelines
- **Product Managers**: Discuss technical trade-offs and feasibility of proposed features

---

## Scrum Master/Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Team Facilitators enable team collaboration, remove impediments, and support process adherence. They foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team on Agile practices and process adherence
- Track team velocity and capacity
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Escalate blockers that require PM or stakeholder intervention

### Goals
- Enable efficient, predictable team delivery
- Foster team collaboration and psychological safety
- Drive continuous process improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- One-on-one coaching and team development
- Blocker escalation to Project Managers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Escalate blockers and resource constraints; provide team capacity data
- **Developers**: Remove impediments and coach on process; facilitate collaboration
- **All Roles**: Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback and continuous improvement
